# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Families in and around Hinganghat (Wardha) planning a wedding, engagement, birthday or other celebration. They scan a printed QR code on a banner, visiting card or at the venue, usually on a mid-range Android phone, often with elders looking at the same screen. Many prefer Marathi. They want to know, in under a minute, what each venue looks like, what each package costs and includes, the booking rules, and how to call or WhatsApp right away.

## Product Purpose

A one-page digital catalog for आई Celebration & BS Lawn's three venues — Aai Celebration Palace (wedding hall), Aai Lawn and BS Lawn — replacing the habit of forwarding poster images on WhatsApp. Success is more calls and WhatsApp enquiries that already name a specific package.

## Brand Personality

Warm, proud, celebratory. A family-run local venue that "takes care of everything" (their tagline: जेव्हा सगळ्या गोष्टी आम्ही सांभाळणार तर तुम्हाला कशाला चिंता). It should feel like a modern Marathi wedding invitation: rich, confident, festive, never cheap or generic.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-made landing pages: cream/beige backgrounds, Cormorant/Playfair headings, centred heading + gold divider repeated on every section, side-stripe callout boxes, fade-in on every section.
- Stock "luxury venue" templates with a dark gradient over a photo and text in the corner.
- Loud, cluttered Indian wedding-portal sites with banners and pop-ups.

## Design Principles

1. The posters are the source of truth — prices, inclusions and rules appear exactly as printed.
2. Marathi first — Devanagari typography must look as good as English, never like a fallback.
3. One tap to enquire — Call and WhatsApp are always within reach.
4. Show the place — real venue photography carries the page (placeholders until real photos arrive).
5. Festive, not fussy — one confident colour story (the logo's maroon and gold), no decoration for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Readable for older family members: body text ≥ 16px, strong contrast, 44px tap targets. Works without saved storage (in-app QR browsers). Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
